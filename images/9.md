# Image 9

Camera disassembled showing a hole drilled on the bottom of the white plastic housing (located under the area where the stand clips in, rendering it completely invisible when reassembled). This hole is drilled to provide a channel where a screwdriver or other long object can be pushed in to push out the front camera cover (the glossy black cover around the lens) from the inside, preventing cosmetic damage to the outside casing that would occur when trying to pry it open from the front.
